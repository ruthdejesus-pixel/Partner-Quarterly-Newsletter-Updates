Product & Engineering
FME 2026.3 is now available Now Live
MCP resources on demand, AI Assist as a Hub plugin, and more
View details ▼

FME 2026.3 shipped September 15 with several significant updates across the platform.

  • FME Flow serves MCP Resources on demand — AI agents can now access file-based workspace outputs directly through MCP, opening up new integration possibilities for your customers.
  • AI Assist moves to MCP as an FME Hub plugin — AI Assist is now independently updatable and decoupled from the FME Form release cycle, meaning faster improvements without waiting for a full release.
  • New opt-in Rejected port on FeatureWriter — A single failed write no longer ends a translation. Partners working with customers on complex data pipelines will find this particularly useful.
  • Apache Iceberg reader — Lakehouse tables on S3, Azure, GCS, and AWS Glue can now be read directly into FME workflows, supporting modern data architecture patterns.
  • Multiple geometry column support for PostGIS and Oracle — Removes the need to pre-create multi-geometry tables outside of FME.
  • Updated platform technical specifications — Refreshed for 2026.3 — useful if you're advising customers on OS support.

📋 Issuing trial licenses — reminder
  • Create a new Deal with the word "Trial" in the Deal name. New customer = Deal Type: New Business. Existing customer = Deal Type: Existing Business.
  • Trial Deals must be used only for trial licenses — do not combine with any other license type.
  • In DealHub, select Trial as the Use Type, then set the Trial Duration (14, 30, or 60 days). Extended periods require CDM approval.

Pro-rated Annual Maintenance credit on Subscription conversions — you have to ask New

When a customer converts to a Subscription with unused Annual Maintenance remaining on assets being rolled in, Safe can calculate a credit based on a daily rate. This is not applied automatically — if it isn't requested, it isn't given. Partners have been leaving customer money on the table without realising.

What to do On every conversion, complete the "Pro-rated AM credit required" field on the Subscription Request Template and list the relevant serial numbers with their current AM end dates.
Licensing
Annual Maintenance on non-production licenses is 25% Correction

The Sales Operations Guide previously showed 20% of SLP for staging licenses. That reflected an old pricing model and has been corrected. Annual Maintenance is 25% of standard list price across all environments — production, staging, development. The purchase discount on a non-production license doesn't reduce its list price, so it doesn't reduce its AM.

What to do If you've quoted AM at 20% on a non-production license recently, revisit it with your CDM.
Licensing
Subscription Deals — do not close until the contract is active Important

For a Subscription, the contract must be fully signed and activated by Safe's Legal team before the deal is marked Closed Won. Closing early creates processing problems for the Orders team and has to be unwound manually.

What to do Once your customer accepts, notify your CDM and submit the Subscription Request Form. After the contract is signed and Legal activates it, you'll receive a confirmation email — only then should the deal be marked Closed Won. This is now documented in the Sales Operations Guide (Step 7) and the Partner HubSpot Walkthrough.
Licensing
Subscription renewal term dates — enter accurately in DealHub Reminder

When creating a DealHub quote for a Subscription renewal, ensure that term dates reflect the actual Subscription term — do not leave them set to the current date. When the quote is saved, the existing term dates on the Deal in HubSpot are overwritten by whatever is entered in DealHub.

Licensing
Multi-year pricing does not create a multi-year renewal obligation Clarification

Multi-year pricing does not create a multi-year renewal obligation. A customer may sign an agreement that outlines pricing for multiple years, but this does not obligate them to renew in future years. For example, a 3-year pricing schedule does not require the customer to renew in Years 2 or 3.

Note This applies to Subscription contracts only. ELA contracts are handled differently — they are committed to all negotiated years and license files are issued for the full term.
